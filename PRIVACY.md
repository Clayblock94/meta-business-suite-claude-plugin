# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

This policy covers the **Meta Business Suite plugin for Claude** ("the plugin"), published at https://github.com/Clayblock94/meta-business-suite-claude-plugin.

## Summary

The plugin does not collect, store, transmit or sell any data. The author runs no server, database or analytics for it.

## What the plugin is

The plugin is a set of instructions (skills) that tell Claude how to operate Meta Business Suite through your browser. It contains no executable code, no API keys and no tracking.

## What data is involved when you use it

When you ask Claude to work in Meta Business Suite, the following happens on your side, not on any server run by the plugin's author:

- **Your Meta account.** You sign in yourself in your own browser. The plugin never asks for, receives or stores your password or any access token.
- **Page content.** To do what you ask, Claude may read what is shown in Business Suite (for example messages, comments, post content and Insights figures) and may write what you approve (captions, scheduled posts, replies).
- **Your messages and files.** Text and media you give Claude to prepare a post are handled in your conversation with Claude.

## Who processes that data

- **Anthropic** processes your conversation and the page content Claude reads, under [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy) and the terms of your Claude plan.
- **Meta** processes anything you publish, schedule or send through Business Suite, under Meta's own terms and privacy policy.

The plugin's author has no access to either.

## Your control

- Claude does not publish, schedule, reply, hide or delete anything without your explicit approval of a preview.
- You can stop at any time, sign out of Business Suite, or uninstall the plugin.
- Messages and comments from third parties may contain personal data. The plugin instructs Claude not to repeat unnecessary personal data in the chat, but you remain responsible for how you use and share that content.

## Children

The plugin is not directed at children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published in this repository with an updated date.
